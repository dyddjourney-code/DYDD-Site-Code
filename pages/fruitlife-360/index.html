Why it matters

A gentle mirror for growth in Christ

The Christian life is not merely about knowing more about ourselves. It is about becoming more like Jesus. FruitLife 360 gives you a simple, pastoral way to reflect on what is becoming visible in your daily life, especially in the ordinary places where love, patience, peace, and self-control are practiced.

You can begin with a self-reflection only, or invite a few people who know you well to offer encouragement and perspective. Either way, the report is designed to be received with humility, hope, and prayer.

This is free

There is no cost to begin. The goal is to help believers listen more carefully to the Spirit, recognize grace already at work, and take one faithful next step.

What you receive

A report built for reflection, not comparison

FruitLife 360 organizes your responses into simple formation language so you can see what is most visible, what is steadily forming, and what may be a growth invitation in this season.

The self-reflection is available when you need a quiet place to begin. When possible, we encourage the peer approach because life in the Body of Christ was never meant to be walked alone: "a cord of three strands is not quickly broken" (Ecclesiastes 4:12).

Galatians 5

The fruit of the Spirit

These are not badges to earn or labels to perform. They are evidence of the Spirit forming the life of Christ in us.

Love

Christlike regard that seeks another person’s good with patience, truth, and sacrifice.

John 13:34-35

Joy

Resilient gladness rooted in God’s presence, goodness, and promises.

John 15:11

Peace

Spirit-formed steadiness that rests in God and makes room for wholeness with others.

Philippians 4:6-7

Patience

Long obedience and gracious endurance when people, process, or timing are slow.

Colossians 3:12-13

Kindness

Tender strength that treats people with dignity, mercy, and practical care.

Ephesians 4:32

Goodness

Moral clarity and active commitment to what reflects God’s character.

Galatians 6:9-10

Faithfulness

Reliable devotion to God, people, and entrusted responsibilities over time.

Lamentations 3:22-23

Gentleness

Humble strength that protects dignity while still telling the truth.

Philippians 4:5

Self-control

Submitted strength that chooses what is faithful when desire, emotion, or pressure rises.

2 Timothy 1:7

Let the report become a conversation with God

FruitLife 360 is not meant to shame, rank worthiness, or measure spiritual value. It is a place to pause, notice, give thanks, and ask the Lord where He is forming Christlike fruit in the next season of your life.
