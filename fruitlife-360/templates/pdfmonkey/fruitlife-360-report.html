How to read this report

A reflection tool, not a spiritual grade.

Scores use a 1-5 visibility scale.

{{ overview_note }}

Self scores reflect how you experience the fruit in your own life. Observer scores reflect how others currently experience that fruit through you. A difference between the two is not failure. It is an invitation to awareness, encouragement, conversation, and formation.

What this report is measuring

Visibility
How consistently each fruit is experienced in ordinary life.
Pressure
How consistently each fruit remains visible under stress, conflict, delay, uncertainty, temptation, disappointment, or fatigue.
Awareness Gap
The difference between self-reflection and observer reflection. Observers higher than self can reveal encouragement; self higher than observers can reveal a growth awareness gap.
Fruit Tiers
The nine fruit are grouped as Most Visible, Steady Forming, and Growth Invitation so the report can point toward gratitude, formation, and next steps.
1Rarely visible
2Occasionally visible
3Somewhat steady
4Usually steady
5Deeply consistent
Awareness gap guide

A gap is the distance between self and observer visibility on the 1-5 scale.

  • 0.0-0.4: generally aligned
  • 0.5-0.9: light difference worth noticing
  • 1.0-1.4: meaningful gap worth discussing
  • 1.5+: major gap for prayerful reflection
Pressure change guide

A pressure change compares normal visibility with visibility under strain.

  • 0.0-0.4: steady under pressure
  • 0.5-0.9: mild pressure sensitivity
  • 1.0-1.4: clear pressure vulnerability
  • 1.5+: significant pressure vulnerability
