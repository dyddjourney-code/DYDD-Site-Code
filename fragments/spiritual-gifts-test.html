How to use your report

Your Gifts Are a Grace to Steward

Use this report as a prayerful beginning, not a final label.

This assessment is a prayerful starting point. It can help you notice patterns in how you serve, encourage, lead, care, build, or strengthen others. It may point to places where the Holy Spirit is already working through your life for the good of the Body of Christ.

But spiritual gifts are best confirmed over time. They become clearer through Scripture, prayer, faithful service, trusted community, and the fruit that grows as you walk with God.

Use your results as an invitation:

  • to pray about what God has placed in you
  • to notice where your gifts are already bearing fruit
  • to ask trusted people what they see in your life
  • to take a first step toward growth, maturity, and service

This report offers insight, not certainty. Guidance, not limits. A beginning, not a final word.

God is still forming you, leading you, and teaching you how to use what He has given for love, service, and His glory.
