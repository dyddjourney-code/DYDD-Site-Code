Hi, I'm Didi, and I am so glad you are here.

Welcome to Discover Your Divine Design. This journey is built around a simple but powerful truth: you were created on purpose, for purpose. Your life is not random. Your personality, your story, your skills, your desires, your gifts, even the places where God has healed you, all of it matters.

One of the verses we love to come back to is Ephesians 2:10: "For we are His workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand, that we should walk in them."

That means you are not trying to invent a purpose from scratch. You are learning to discover, receive, and walk in the good works God has already prepared for you.

As you move through this experience, you will meet a team of companions. Each one reflects part of the way God designs people to serve, lead, care, create, build, and sustain. You will meet Architects who bring vision and structure, Artisans who make truth visible and meaningful, Shepherds who help people feel safe and seen, and Stewards who protect what matters and help it last.

They are not here to label you or put you in a box. They are here to help you notice what God has placed in you, how He has shaped you, and how your story can become a doorway into ministry and service.

So take a breath. You do not have to have everything figured out today. You just need to be willing to pay attention, tell the truth, and let God show you what He has been weaving together all along.

I am excited you are here. Let's begin this journey together.
