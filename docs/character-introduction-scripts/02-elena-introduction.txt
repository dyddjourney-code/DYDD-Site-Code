Hi, I'm Elena.

I grew up in a military family, so change was part of my life from the beginning. We moved often, and I had to learn how to enter new places, read the room, build trust, and find belonging quickly. That shaped me. It taught me to look for the people who might be overlooked and to ask what could be created to help them thrive.

I studied communications and nonprofit leadership, and I spent years launching community initiatives and mentoring young leaders. Along the way, I learned something important: vision without care can become machinery, but care without structure can fade before it has the chance to bless people.

In DesignID language, I am an Architect first and a Shepherd second. My integrative reflection is Innovator. I see possibilities, but I also feel deeply aware of the people affected by those possibilities. I love creating new pathways, not just because new is exciting, but because the right pathway can help people heal, belong, and step into purpose.

My spiritual gifts include apostleship, leadership, exhortation, faith, and discernment. I love encouraging people to move forward with courage when God is inviting them into something bigger than what they currently see.

My growth edge is learning which burdens are mine to carry. Sometimes compassion makes me want to build a solution for every person I see hurting. Jesus keeps teaching me that I am called to obey, not rescue.

My niche is launching compassionate pathways for people who need direction. I am excited to help you imagine what could be built through your life, and to help you take courageous next steps with both faith and care.
