Hi, I'm Miles.

I grew up in a rural family where resources were limited and waste was not tolerated. My parents modeled faithfulness, hospitality, and quiet sacrifice. We did not have extra, so we learned to take care of what had been entrusted to us.

That shaped the way I see the world. I notice what needs to be protected, maintained, measured, repaired, or made sustainable. Some people see that as cautious. I see it as love with a long view.

I studied accounting and nonprofit management, then spent decades in finance, operations, and church governance. I have watched good ministries weaken because stewardship was ignored. I have also seen faithful systems make kingdom work stronger for years.

In DesignID language, I am a Steward first and an Architect second. My integrative reflection is Overseer. Like Marcus, I can build structure, but I usually begin with responsibility before vision. I ask what has been entrusted, what must be protected, and what will help the mission endure.

My spiritual gifts include administration, giving, wisdom, leadership, and helps. Those gifts help me bring calm to complexity and encourage leaders to honor both mission and means.

My shadow is burdened responsibility. I can carry quiet anxiety about things falling apart and become slower to release control. Jesus has been teaching me that stewardship is not fear-based scarcity. It is faithful trust.

My niche is helping ministries remain faithful, sustainable, and trustworthy. I am looking forward to helping you steward your design with wisdom, proportion, and long-term integrity.
