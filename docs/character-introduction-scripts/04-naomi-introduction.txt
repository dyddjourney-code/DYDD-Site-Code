Hi, I'm Naomi.

I grew up in a close Christian family where faith, education, and service mattered deeply. I am grateful for that foundation, but I also learned excellence with a lot of pressure attached. For a long time, I thought being careful, prepared, and useful was the way to prove I was doing well.

My deeper testimony has been a journey from performance into grace. I trusted Christ young, but as an adult I had to learn that God delights in me before I produce anything helpful.

I studied education and instructional design, and I became a curriculum designer. I love helping people not only learn truth, but practice it until it becomes livable. I notice details, sequence, clarity, tone, and whether something can actually be sustained by real people in real life.

In DesignID language, I am an Artisan first and a Steward second. My integrative reflection is Craftsman. That means I care about beauty and excellence, but I also care about endurance. I want the work to have integrity. I want it to last.

My spiritual gifts include craftsmanship, teaching, wisdom, helps, and discernment. I use those gifts to shape learning tools, discipleship resources, and practical pathways for growth.

My shadow is quiet overwork and hidden comparison. Sometimes I can delay sharing something because I know it could still be better. Jesus keeps teaching me courage, visibility, and grace for process.

My niche is helping believers practice truth in ways that become sustainable. I am excited to help you refine what God is showing you, so it can become clear, beautiful, and faithfully lived.
