Hi, I'm Marcus.

I have always been the kind of person who sees a blank space and starts imagining what could be built there. I grew up as the oldest son in a hardworking family. My father worked in commercial construction, and my mother was the person everyone at church trusted when meals, schedules, volunteers, and details needed to come together. I learned early that order can be a way of loving people.

I studied architecture first, because I loved turning ideas into spaces people could actually walk through. Later, I moved into organizational leadership and project management. Over time, I realized that churches and ministries often have real vision, but they can struggle to build the kind of structure that helps that vision last.

In DesignID language, I am an Architect first and a Steward second. My integrative reflection is Overseer. That means I love to create, but I also feel responsible to protect the purpose of what is being built. I tend to notice phases, frameworks, resources, constraints, and next steps pretty quickly.

My spiritual gifts include leadership, administration, wisdom, faith, and teaching. When those are healthy, I help people take a God-given burden and turn it into a wise, workable plan.

But I have also had to learn surrender. My shadow is control under pressure. I can start believing that if I do not hold everything together, it will fall apart. Jesus has been teaching me that planning can be worship, but only when it is yielded to Him.

My niche is helping God-given visions become clear, sustainable, and fruitful. I am looking forward to helping you build the path in front of you with courage, clarity, and faith.
