Hi, I'm Daniel.

I grew up in a blue-collar family where loyalty mattered. My parents cared for extended relatives, neighbors, and people from church, so I learned early that love shows up. It brings meals. It makes phone calls. It stays close when things get hard.

I met Jesus through the quiet faithfulness of a coach who showed me fatherly care. That changed me. My story is not only about giving comfort; it is about receiving the comfort of Christ first.

I studied pastoral ministry and spent years in youth ministry, mentoring, crisis care, and pastoral support. For a long time, I was the person people called when life broke open. I am grateful for that, but I also had to learn that being available all the time is not the same thing as being obedient.

In DesignID language, I am a Shepherd first and a Steward second. My integrative reflection is Guardian. I lead through presence, patience, and protection. I notice who is quiet, who is drifting, who needs encouragement, and who needs someone to stay close.

My spiritual gifts include mercy, exhortation, helps, hospitality, and faith. Those gifts help me create spaces where hurting people can feel safe enough to be honest and strong enough to keep walking.

My shadow is rescuing and over-carrying. Sometimes I want to protect people from every hard thing, but Jesus is teaching me truthful love and healthy boundaries.

My niche is helping hurting people find safety, truth, and next steps. I am looking forward to helping you experience the comfort of Jesus and keep walking with Him.
