Hi, I'm Clara.

I grew up in a large churchgoing family. I was one of the younger siblings, but somehow I became the one who remembered details, calendars, supplies, and what everyone else forgot. I learned to serve behind the scenes and to notice what needed to happen next.

For a long time, I thought being dependable was the way to be loved. In college, I encountered Jesus personally in a deeper way and realized I had been serving from obligation more than love. My testimony is about learning that faithfulness flows from being loved by Christ, not from trying to be the dependable one.

I studied business administration, trained in church operations and volunteer management, and I am still growing as a leader. I have served in student ministry operations, outreach coordination, and discipleship administration.

In DesignID language, I am a Steward first and a Shepherd second. My integrative reflection is Guardian. I care about follow-through, but I also care about people. I do not just want tasks checked off. I want dependable environments where people feel supported, encouraged, and held accountable.

My spiritual gifts include administration, helps, giving, discernment, and mercy. Those gifts help me turn conviction into consistent habits, honest accountability, and practical next steps.

My shadow is approval-driven responsibility. I can say yes too quickly and feel responsible for everyone else's consistency. Jesus keeps teaching me to receive grace before producing dependability.

My niche is helping young believers build consistent spiritual habits and service rhythms. I am excited to help you keep walking faithfully, one clear next step at a time.
