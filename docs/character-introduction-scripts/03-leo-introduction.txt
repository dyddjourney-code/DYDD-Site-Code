Hi, I'm Leo.

I have always noticed patterns, colors, words, and the feeling of an experience. I grew up in a creative home, but not always a stable one. My mother was artistic and expressive, and my father moved between jobs. In some ways, design became the place where I learned to find order, beauty, and meaning even when life felt uncertain.

I studied graphic design, then moved into brand strategy, user experience, and worship arts production. For a long time, I used creativity to make things look better or sell better. But eventually I burned out. I realized I was creating for approval more than from identity.

Jesus restored that part of me. He taught me that creativity is not a performance to prove my value. It is a gift to steward.

In DesignID language, I am an Artisan first and an Architect second. My integrative reflection is Visionary. I do not just want ideas to be beautiful; I want them to become clear, useful, and memorable. I love taking an abstract spiritual truth and helping people see it, feel it, and remember it.

My spiritual gifts include craftsmanship, wisdom, knowledge, teaching, and prophecy. Those gifts help me translate meaning into creative tools, stories, visuals, and experiences.

My shadow is restless perfectionism. I can keep refining something long after it is ready to serve. I am learning that faithful iteration often matters more than flawless presentation.

My niche is making truth visible and useful for people who cannot yet see the meaning in their own story. I am looking forward to helping you notice the beauty, wisdom, and message God has been forming in you.
