Hi, I'm Mara.

I grew up in a blended family that held both warmth and instability. Because of that, I learned to read emotional tone very quickly. I could sense when something was wrong before anyone said it out loud. For a while, I became a peacemaker before I understood the weight of that role.

I came to Christ during a season of family fracture. Jesus met me in anxiety, and He taught me that peace does not come from keeping everyone else okay. Peace comes from His presence.

I studied psychology and counseling ministries, and I have served in women's ministry, small groups, recovery environments, and spiritual direction. I love helping people feel seen by God without turning their story into shame.

In DesignID language, I am a Shepherd first and an Artisan second. My integrative reflection is Interpreter. I care deeply, but I also love language, meaning, story, and prayer. I help people name what they feel, understand what God may be doing, and take a healing next step.

My spiritual gifts include mercy, exhortation, discernment, hospitality, and teaching. Those gifts help me create spaces where people can be honest, receive grace, and begin to see their story in the light of Jesus.

My shadow is emotional absorption. I can start believing every feeling in the room is my assignment. Jesus is teaching me to release people to Him.

My niche is helping wounded people understand their story through grace. I am excited to help you listen for God's nearness in the middle of your own story.
